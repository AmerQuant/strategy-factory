# ADR-003: Polars in data, pandas at the edges, NumPy in the engine

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-19

## Decision

Polars در لایهٔ داده، pandas در لبه‌ها، NumPy در موتور

_English:_ Polars in the data layer, pandas only at the edges (reports, stats libraries), NumPy in the engine.

_Source: design document v1.0, §14 (decision log); rationale in §2._
